Welcome to Snapline

Snapline tunes a vocal in real time and keeps it sounding human.

Click Continue, then Install. Your Mac will ask for your password once. That is all.

This installs two plug-in formats, so it works in every common music app:

  • Audio Unit, for Logic Pro, GarageBand and MainStage
  • VST3, for FL Studio, Ableton Live, Reaper, Cubase, Bitwig, Studio One and others

Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
