Snapline is installed

Close this window and open your music app. Put Snapline on a vocal track as an effect.

  • If you had your music app open, quit it and open it again.
  • If Snapline is not in the list, rescan your plug-ins in the app's settings (Logic Pro does this by itself when it starts).
  • For a finished recording use Studio mode. For hearing yourself while you record, use Live.

To remove Snapline later, delete Snapline.component from /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components and Snapline.vst3 from /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3.
